# Clipboard Router Privacy Policy

Effective: August 21, 2026

Clipboard Router is designed to keep clipboard work on your Mac. The core
clipboard history, saved clips, notes, folders, Private Sessions, Sensitive
Review, and Vault operate locally. Clipboard Router does not sell personal
information, track people across apps or websites, or include an analytics
transport.

Clipboard content is processed to provide app functionality. Private Session
clips remain in memory. Vault items are encrypted and require authentication
before they are revealed. Clipboard Router does not automatically submit a
prompt, message, CRM update, or other external action.

Some optional features can transmit data only after the user enables or invokes
them. These include opening a reviewed handoff in another application, using a
user-configured online service, or enabling a platform sync capability in a
build that supports it. The destination service's privacy terms apply to data
the user chooses to send there.

Calendar, Contacts, and Location permissions are requested only for explicit
user-selected actions. Denying those permissions does not prevent ordinary
clipboard capture, search, saving, notes, folders, Private Sessions, or Vault
use.

For privacy questions, use the contact method in [SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md).
