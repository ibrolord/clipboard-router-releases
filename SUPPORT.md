# Clipboard Router Support

For help, bug reports, or privacy questions, open an issue in the
[Clipboard Router release repository](https://github.com/ibrolord/clipboard-router-releases/issues).

When reporting a problem, include the Clipboard Router version, macOS version,
Mac model, and steps to reproduce. Do not include clipboard contents, license
keys, credentials, or other secrets.

Clipboard Router 0.1.0 supports macOS 14 Sonoma or later on Apple Silicon.
